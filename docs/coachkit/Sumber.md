# Sumber cara mengukur (Fase AD, T-2400)

Every step in the measuring guide (`parts/app/21-cara-ukur.part`, `UKUR_STEPS` / `UKUR_UMUM`) follows one of these protocols. Change a step only together with this table.

| ID | Ukuran | Langkah di aplikasi (ringkas) | Protokol | Sumber |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| U01 | Pinggang | tengah antara tulang rusuk terbawah dan puncak tulang pinggul, akhir napas keluar biasa, lengan rileks | WHO STEPS, Step 2 (waist circumference) | WHO, *Waist circumference and waist–hip ratio: report of a WHO expert consultation* (2008/2011), https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241501491; summarised in the UK MRC measurement toolkit, https://measurement-toolkit.org/anthropometry/objective-methods/simple-measures-circumference |
| U02 | Pinggul | bagian bokong paling lebar, kaki rapat, lengan rileks | WHO STEPS, Step 2 (hip circumference) | same as U01 |
| U03 | Perut | setinggi pusar, akhir napas keluar biasa | common practice (umbilical girth); **not** a WHO/ISAK site — the app keeps it because clients already log it | — |
| U04 | Lengan | lengan tergantung rileks, titik tengah ujung bahu (acromiale) – siku (radiale), pita mendatar | ISAK, arm girth relaxed at mid-acromiale-radiale | ISAK, *International Standards for Anthropometric Assessment* (manual); checked against secondary copies, wording to confirm against the official manual |
| U05 | Dada | tengah dada (mesosternale) lewat bawah ketiak, akhir napas keluar biasa | ISAK, chest girth at mesosternale | ISAK manual (as U04) |
| U06 | Paha | ±1 cm di bawah lipatan bokong, berat di kedua kaki | ISAK, thigh girth 1 cm below the gluteal fold | ISAK manual (as U04) |
| U07 | Berat | pagi, setelah ke kamar kecil, sebelum makan, baju tipis, timbangan sama | common practice for repeat weighing (same conditions each time) | — |
| U08 | Umum | waktu sama, pita menempel tanpa menekan, ukur dua kali dan rata-rata, sisi dan pengukur sama | WHO / ISAK general measuring rules (tape on skin, read at the end of a normal breath out, repeat measures) | as U01 and U04 |

"Setinggi puting" in U05 is a plain-language pointer to mesosternale level for most adults; the app says "tengah dada" first.

Check before changing the text: the steps are short on purpose (three lines, Indonesian, neutral, no "kamu").
